# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps: being kind and patient, respecting different opinions and experience, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, owning mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, promoting tokens or trading schemes in project spaces, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the [Discord](https://discord.gg/grokbot) (message a moderator) or by opening a private security advisory on the repository if the report is sensitive. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful.
